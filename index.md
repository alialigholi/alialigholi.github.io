## About Me

My full first name is Mohammadali, but I go by Ali. I’m a 5th-year PhD student in pure mathematics at UW–Madison, advised by [Laurentiu Maxim](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~lmaxim/). I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in pure mathematics from Sharif University of Technology in Tehran, Iran, in 2022.
