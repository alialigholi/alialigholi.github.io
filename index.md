## About Me

My full first name is Mohammadali, but I go by Ali. I’m a PhD student in pure mathematics at [UW–Madison](https://www.math.wisc.edu/), advised by [Laurentiu Maxim](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~lmaxim/).
